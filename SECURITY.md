# Security Policy

This repository holds a policy brief (a Markdown document). It has no code
and no executable surface, so most "security issues" here mean either a
compromised repository or something that misleads the reader about what
Slid Phi Labs said or built.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please do not open a public issue for security problems.

- Email: Corey@slidphilabs.com with the subject line
  `super-intelligence-force-brief security`
- Or use GitHub's private vulnerability reporting on this repository
  (Security tab, "Report a vulnerability")

Include what you found, where, and how to reproduce it. You can expect an
acknowledgement within 3 business days. We will keep you updated while we
investigate and credit you unless you prefer to stay anonymous.

## In scope

- Signs the repository itself was compromised (tampered history, unrecognized
  commits, forged tags or releases)
- Impersonation: content falsely attributed to Slid Phi Labs or Corey Tasz
- Links in the brief that were swapped to point somewhere malicious

## Out of scope

- Disagreement with the brief's position (open an issue instead)
- Social engineering, spam, or denial-of-service
- Vulnerabilities in third-party sites the brief links to
